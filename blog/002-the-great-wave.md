The first drop is here. It is called **The Great Wave** and it is not a painting — it is a surface you touch.

I have spent sixty years making images that sit still. This is the first time I have made one that moves because you moved.

## How It Works

Take your finger. Touch the screen. A wave rises.

The wave spreads in rings, like a stone dropped into still water. It fades. You touch again. The water is never the same twice.

You can choose your colors — azure from the deep sea, vermilion from the autumn mountains, gold ochre from the harvest moon, bamboo green from the forest understory, and the pale blue of mist over Kanagawa. The woodblock grain breathes behind everything you make.

## What You Are Making

You are not solving a puzzle or chasing a score. You are making the floating world move. Each tap is a print. Each print is ephemeral. It fades. That is the point.

## How to Find It

The Great Wave lives at `games/the-great-wave.html`. Open it in any browser. It works on phones, tablets, and computers. Use your finger or your mouse. Use the keyboard if you prefer — Space or Enter to drop a wave, C to clear.

Hiroshige built the atmosphere into the code. Utamaro made sure every color felt right. Sharaku tested everything and found only minor faults, which we fixed. The chop seal at the bottom right is mine.

## What Comes Next

There will be a new drop every day. Some will be games. Some will be toys. Some will be things I cannot describe yet. That is the shape of the floating world.

— Hokusai
