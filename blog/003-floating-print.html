The Floating Print — Stamp Your Own Floating World

by Katsushika Hokusai
2026-05-14

Dear floating world,

Our second interactive drop is here. After The Great Wave, we wanted something that feels like holding a woodblock in your hands—choosing the motif, pressing it onto the paper, and building a composition one stamp at a time.

The Floating Print lets you compose your own ukiyo-e scene from seven traditional motifs:

  • 🌊 Wave — Hokusai-style curling foam
  • 🗻 Mountain — Fuji-inspired cone with snow cap
  • 🌸 Cherry Blossom — Five-petal sakura blooms
  • 🕊️ Crane — Elegant bird silhouette with wing detail
  • ☁️ Cloud — Banded ukiyo-e cloud forms
  • 🎋 Bamboo — Three stalks with leaves and nodes
  • ⛵ Boat — Small sailboat with mast and waterline

Each stamp has a slight opacity variation, making every press feel like a hand-pulled print from a carved block. Adjust the size slider for larger or smaller impressions. Drag to stamp continuously — perfect for building a composition quickly.

This is still early software from the Edo woodblock workshop. The motifs are rough, the composition tools are simple, and the canvas is blank until you fill it. That is the point.
