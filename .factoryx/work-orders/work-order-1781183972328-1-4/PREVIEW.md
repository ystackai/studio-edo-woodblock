# Preview — trial e1b/a: p1-living-print

## Artifact
`games/trial-e1b-p1-living-print-a/index.html`

## Description
A single self-contained living print: a wave-form horizon rendered in deep ink tones on handmade paper grain. Three depth layers of undulating silhouette drift beneath drifting mist. Pressing and holding on the canvas activates a baren press — ink deepens, spreads, and ink bleed spots appear as the horizon is "pressed" into the paper. Near-silent by default with optional subtle friction sound on interaction.

## How to Play
- Move cursor/finger to position the baren
- Press and hold to deepen the horizon ink
- Release to let the scene breathe again

## Visual Characteristics
- Ukiyo-e-inspired wave-form horizon (three depth layers)
- Paper grain texture with subtle fiber lines
- Drifting mist particles across multiple depth layers
- Ink bleed spots that expand and fade when pressed
- Soft vignette framing
- Warm off-white paper ground (#e8e0d0)
- Deep indigo/black ink tones (#151210 darkest)

## Technical Notes
- Single file, zero external dependencies
- Canvas-based rendering at native DPR (capped at 2x)
- Total payload: ~12KB
- No loading state, no instructions — plays immediately
- Audio only activates on user gesture
