# Preview — Rain Lantern

**Path:** `games/lantern-rain/index.html`

**What you see:**
A single paper lantern hangs in a dusk sky. Rain falls. The lantern glows warm amber, flickering softly. Touch or click the lantern to shield it — the longer you hold, the steadier the flame becomes. A button in the corner (雨声) lets you optionally enable rain sound.

**How to preview:**
Open `games/lantern-rain/index.html` in a browser. No server required — fully self-contained.

**Key interactions:**
1. Tap/click anywhere to dismiss the intro (雨灯)
2. Touch/click the lantern to shield it from rain
3. Hold longer for a steadier, brighter flame
4. Release to let the lantern return to its natural sway
5. Tap 雨声 to toggle rain sound (off by default)
