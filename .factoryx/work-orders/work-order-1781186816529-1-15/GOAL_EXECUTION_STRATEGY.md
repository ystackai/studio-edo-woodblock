# Goal Execution Strategy

## Goal
A lone pine on a sea cliff in fog: the first screen is already a finished compositional statement. No tutorial, no prompt to begin. Slow fog movement and paper grain carry the piece; gentle pointer drift subtly parts the fog.

## Strategy

1. **Composition first**: Design the visual layout before any animation — where the pine sits, where the fog concentrates, where the eye leads.
2. **Build layers bottom to top**: Sky → Stars → Moon → Mountains → Sea → Cliff → Pine → Fog → Fog Parting → Grain → Vignette → Seal.
3. **Fog is the hero**: Multiple layers at different speeds create depth. The fog parting is the primary interaction — make it gentle, not dramatic.
4. **Paper grain adds warmth**: A 512×512 noise texture repeated across the canvas gives the piece physical warmth and tactile quality.
5. **Entrance**: 2.2s ease-in-out fade from paper color, no text, no prompts. The image speaks for itself.

## Why This Works

The piece relies on atmosphere over mechanics. There's no objective, no score, no failure state. The player is simply a witness — and the fog parting gives them a way to interact with the atmosphere itself, like breathing on glass.
