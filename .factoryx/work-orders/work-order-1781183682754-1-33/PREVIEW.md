# Preview: Koi Breath (e1b/b)

## Entry point
`games/trial-e1b-p4-koi-breath-b/index.html`

## How to experience
1. Open the page in any modern browser
2. Press and hold (mouse click or touch) on the pond surface
3. Hold patiently for at least 700ms to trigger an ink bloom
4. Move while holding to draw ink across the pond
5. Release to let the ink slowly fade

## What you'll see
- A deep koi pond with animated caustic light patterns
- Several koi fish swimming naturally
- When you hold, dark ink tendrils bloom outward with organic, curling motion
- A pulsing glow at your press point while holding
- The ink slowly fades after you release
- A depth counter (水深) appears showing accumulated ink

## Constraints met
- Single self-contained file, no external dependencies
- Touch + mouse/keyboard support
- Responsive layout for any screen size
- All assets generated procedurally (~16KB)
- No audio (chosen silence)
