# Preview — Living Print: Wave Horizon

**Preview path:** `games/trial-p1-living-print-b/index.html`

**What you'll see:** A single, complete composition on first sight. Warm handmade paper grain fills the screen. A deep indigo wave-form horizon breathes gently in the lower half. Mist drifts across the scene. A pale sun/moon hangs in the upper sky. Small birds silhouette against the mist.

**Interaction:** Press and hold anywhere on the canvas. The wave deepens as if you're pressing a baren onto wet paper — slight resistance, then the ink sinks deeper. Ripple rings propagate outward. Birds scatter gently. Ink bloom particles appear near the wave. Release and everything slowly relaxes.

**Audio:** Near-silent by default. First press activates barely-there wind noise and a low hum. Volume modulates with press depth.

**Controls:** Mouse click/hold, touch press/hold, or spacebar.
