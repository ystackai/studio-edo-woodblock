# Preview

**Entrypoint:** `games/ukiyo-e-printer/index.html`  
**Preview path:** `games/ukiyo-e-printer/index.html`

## How to Preview

1. Serve the repo root: `npx http-server . -p 8080` or any static server
2. Open: `http://localhost:8080/games/ukiyo-e-printer/index.html`
3. Or open `index.html` directly in a browser

## What to Experience

1. **Opening:** Wait for the title to fade in — "Touch the paper. Breathe upon it."
2. **First touch:** Click once to start. Feel the subtle paper darken.
3. **Stroke:** Drag slowly across the paper. Notice the resistance — the cursor drags behind your pointer.
4. **Hold:** Press and hold for 1–2 seconds. Watch the ink ring expand with fiber displacement and breath vapor.
5. **Friction:** Click rapidly — notice how marks become fainter. The paper "saturates."
6. **Patience:** Wait a moment, then press again. The paper remembers and slowly recovers.
7. **Finish:** After several marks, press "FINISH" to see the seal stamp.
