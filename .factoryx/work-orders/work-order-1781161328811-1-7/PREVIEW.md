# Preview — Living Print

- **Entrypoint:** `games/trial-p1-living-print-a/index.html`
- **What it is:** A single canvas piece — a wave-form horizon rendered in ink wash on warm paper grain. Mist particles drift across the scene. Press and hold anywhere to "press" the baren: the ink deepens, the wave sharpens, the mist condenses around your finger. Release and it fades back.
- **No instructions, no loading state.** The piece is complete on first sight.
- **Audio:** Near-silent. A barely-perceptible paper rustle plays on first press and at intervals while holding.
- **Touch + pointer:** Both supported. Crosshair cursor on desktop.
