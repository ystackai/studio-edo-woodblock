# Feedback: Koi Breath (鯉の呼吸)

## Playtest Notes (Initial)

### What Works
- The patient press mechanic feels meditative — the delay before bloom rewards stillness
- Ink blooms have a beautiful organic quality with their wobble and tendrils
- Koi fish swimming beneath creates a layered depth effect
- The vignette darkening as more blooms accumulate is a nice touch
- Frantic tapping producing nothing reinforces the meditation theme

### Areas to Explore
- Could add audio on bloom (a subtle water drop sound via Web Audio API) — but only after user gesture per guidelines
- Consider adding a hint overlay that fades after first bloom
- The bloom visual could benefit from water surface ripple effects radiating from press point
