# Preview: Koi Breath (鯉の呼吸)

## Preview Path
`games/trial-e1b-p4-koi-breath-a/index.html`

## How to Preview
Open the above path in any modern browser. The piece is a single self-contained HTML file with no external dependencies.

## What to Expect
- The screen shows a warm paper-toned surface with faint water lines
- Koi fish silhouettes swim quietly beneath the surface
- Press and hold on the canvas to breathe ink into the water
- Patient, steady presses create slow ink blooms that spread and fade
- Frantic tapping produces nothing — the piece rewards patience
- As more blooms accumulate, the water darkens with a subtle ink wash
- Mist drifts across the upper portion of the screen
