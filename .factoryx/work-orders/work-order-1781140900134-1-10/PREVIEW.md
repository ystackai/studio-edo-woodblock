# Preview — Koi Breath

**Entrypoint:** `games/trial-p4-koi-breath-a/index.html`

**What you see:** A warm paper-toned pond surface with subtle koi silhouettes, drifting mist, and floating leaves. Press and hold gently to bloom ink into the water. The longer you hold, the more intricate the bloom.

**The one gesture:** Press and hold. Patient hands create layered ink blooms. Frantic tapping is ignored — the piece rewards stillness.

**Visual style:** Ukiyo-e house style — sumi black, deep indigo, faded vermilion on handmade paper.
