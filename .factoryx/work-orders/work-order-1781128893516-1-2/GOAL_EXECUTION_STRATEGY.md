# Goal Execution Strategy

**One print, one verb, one space.**

- The piece opens complete — a wave horizon in indigo ink on warm paper grain, with mist drifting.
- The single verb is "press" — holding down anywhere deepens the ink, like pressing a baren on wet print.
- No menus, no instructions, no loading. The image IS the interface.
- Sound is sparse: a breath on first touch, a click on press. Everything else is silence.
- The goal is not to entertain but to hold attention — the piece is done when it feels like a real print.
