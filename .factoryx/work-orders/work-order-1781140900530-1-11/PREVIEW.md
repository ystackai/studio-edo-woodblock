# Preview — p4-koi-breath

**Entrypoint:** `games/trial-p4-koi-breath-b/index.html`

**What you see:** A warm paper-toned surface representing a koi pond. Six koi fish swim in silhouette across the screen in varying colors (sumi-dark, beni-vermilion, karasugigo-silver, kin-golden). Subtle water ripple lines move across the surface. Mist gathers at the edges.

**What you do:** Press and hold anywhere on the pond. After a brief pause, ink begins to bloom outward — like a drop of sumi ink hitting water. The longer you hold, the larger and more complex the bloom becomes, with multiple layers of ink wash radiating outward. Koi fish will swim away from fresh ink. Release and the ink slowly fades like a memory.

**Try this:** Hold gently for 3-5 seconds. Watch how the bloom grows. Notice the koi scatter. Hold somewhere else and see the second bloom interact with the first.
