# Preview — p3-lantern-rain

**Preview path**: `games/lantern-rain/`

**What to expect:**
- A single paper lantern hanging in the dusk rain
- Rain streaks across the canvas
- Warm amber glow pulsing through the lantern's wet paper
- Click/tap to place a hand over the lantern (shields it from rain briefly)
- The longer you hold, the steadier the flame becomes
- Release and the rain returns; the flame slowly drifts again
- Click "Rain" button (after first interaction) to optionally hear rain sounds
- No score, no objectives — just atmosphere
