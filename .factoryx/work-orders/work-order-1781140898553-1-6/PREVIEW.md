# Preview: A Lone Pine on the Sea Cliff

**Path:** `games/trial-p2-quiet-opening-a/index.html`

## What You See

A single screen, already a finished compositional statement. No tutorial. No prompt. Just:

- A warm washi-paper ground with fiber grain and subtle creases
- Distant mountains rendered in faded ink-wash tones
- An indigo sea with gentle wave lines
- A dark cliff rising from the water on the right
- A lone pine, its trunk curved by wind, branches sweeping toward the sea
- A smaller companion pine on the cliff edge
- Three layers of fog, drifting at different speeds across the scene
- Distant birds, barely there
- A soft vignette at the edges

## Interaction

Move your cursor (or finger) across the screen. The fog parts gently around your pointer, revealing the cliff and sea beneath. The effect is subtle — you are breathing on the print, not erasing it.

## Technical

- Single self-contained HTML file (~18 KB)
- Six stacked canvas layers: paper, scene, 3 fog layers, grain, vignette
- No external assets or network dependencies
- Runs at 60fps; only fog layers animate each frame
- Touch + mouse supported
- Responsive to window resize
