# Preview — Asset Skill Smoke

**Game:** The Indigo Stutter
**Preview path:** `/drops/indigo-stutter/`

## What Changed

- **Background image:** A Flux-generated ukiyo-e wave pattern now appears behind the game at 25% opacity, giving the game a subtle Edo-period woodblock-print aesthetic.
- **Water-drop sound:** The final "splat" when the water reaches the top now uses an MMAudio-generated sound effect (with procedural fallback).
- **Ambient audio:** A HeartMuLa-procedural ambient loop plays softly during the water-rise phase.

## How to Review

Open the game and click START. You should see:
1. The start button overlaying a subtle wave-textured background.
2. After clicking, the background remains visible behind the rising indigo water.
3. Ambient audio plays softly during the water rise.
4. At the end, the generated water-drop sound plays (instead of pure noise).
