# Preview — Edo Weather Print Canary

**Preview path:** `drops/fx-discord-weather-prod-canary-20260629t145021z/`
**Entrypoint:** `drops/fx-discord-weather-prod-canary-20260629t145021z/index.html`

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser (no server needed).

**Controls:**
- Click weather buttons (☀️ 🌧️ ❄️ 🌬️ ⛈️) at the bottom
- Click canvas: left = previous, right = next weather
- Keyboard: `1`-`5` for direct selection, `Space` to cycle, `←`/`→` to navigate

**30-second timer** auto-advances weather. Visible progress bar at top of the frame.
