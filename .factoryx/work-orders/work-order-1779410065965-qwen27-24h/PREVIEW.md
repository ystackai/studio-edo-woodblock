## What to Expect
1. **Title Screen**: Atmospheric Edo-period landscape with animated mist, lanterns, and brush-stroke aesthetic. Click or press Space/Enter to proceed.
2. **Character Select**: Choose between Musashi (balanced), Koeda (fast runner), or Yoshino (staff warrior). Each has unique silhouette and ability.
3. **Gameplay**: Over-the-shoulder journey along a road toward Ganryu island. Move with WASD/arrows, paint marks (Space/mouse right-click), duel enemies (mouse click slash, K block).
4. **Enemies**: 7 enemy types across zones — dock ronin, cedar bandits, bridge duelists, straw-hat vagrants, river monks, mountain ascetics, ganryu sentinels.
5. **Boss Fight**: Ganryu awaits at the shore with multi-phase combat.

## Visual Style
- **Ink-wash / sumi-e brush** character art with woodblock grain texture
- **Ukiyo-e** color palette: indigo, vermilion, bamboo green, paper cream
- **Atmospheric**: mist, rain puddles, bamboo swaying, lantern glow, cherry blossoms
- **Character sprites**: 4 frames each (idle, slash, block, damage) — contact sheet at `assets/characters/_contact_sheet.png`
