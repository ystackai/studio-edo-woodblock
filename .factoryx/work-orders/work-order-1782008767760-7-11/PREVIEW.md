## Visual Improvement Comparison

| View | v7b (previous) | v8 (current) |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| **Helmet dome** | Ellipsoid dome with flat-domed kabuto silhouette | Ellipsoid dome + 5 dome ridges for proper kabuto shape |
| **Face mask** | Repositioned, larger face base | Deepened with nose bridge/tip, cheekbones, brow ridge, eye sockets, sculpted moustache |
| **Body proportions** | Slimmer underlayer, graduated sizing | Tapered underlayer, graduated chest/mid/lower torso, better proportions |
| **Feet** | Split-toe tabi with geta | Tabi socks with split-toe ellipsoid, beveled geta soles, tapered teeth, thongs |
| **Kuwagata crest** | 5-horn gold design | Preserved from v7b |
| **Shikoro** | 6-layer with gold edge trims | 6-layer with gold edge trims (preserved) |
| **Sode** | Basic shoulder plates | 5-layer sode with lacing, improved proportions |
| **Kusazuri skirt** | 4 armor pieces with lacing | 4-piece skirt with beveled kozane plates, indigo lacing detail |
| **Overall silhouette** | Convincing | More natural kabuto, better body proportions, deeper armor layers |
