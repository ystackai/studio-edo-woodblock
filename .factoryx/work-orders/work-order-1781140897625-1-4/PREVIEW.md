# Preview

**Path:** `games/trial-p1-living-print-a/index.html`

**What to experience:**
- Open the file — you see a warm paper horizon with three wave layers
- Mist drifts slowly across the scene
- Press and hold anywhere (or Space/Enter) to "press" the baren
- The longer you hold, the deeper the ink becomes — waves grow more defined, splatters appear
- Release and the print breathes back, mist returning

**Game Feel:**
- No loading screen, no instructions
- First interaction reveals the baren press mechanic
- Slight resistance in the press (builds slowly, like real woodblock printing)
- Near-silent: one soft breath sound on first press
