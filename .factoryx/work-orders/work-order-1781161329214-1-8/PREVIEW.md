# Preview

**Path:** `games/trial-p2-quiet-opening-b/index.html`

Open directly in any modern browser. No server required — fully self-contained.

**What you see:** A lone pine on a sea cliff, shrouded in fog. The composition is left-weighted (cliff + pine) with vast sea and sky to the right. Two tiny birds drift in the distance.

**What you do:** Move your cursor (or finger) across the scene. The fog parts gently where you touch, as if breathing on the woodblock print. Remove your hand and the fog slowly closes.
