# PREVIEW — Lantern Tide

## Preview URL
`games/lantern-tide/index.html`

## How to play
1. Open `games/lantern-tide/index.html` in a browser (or serve via local HTTP server).
2. Click anywhere or press Space to start — this also unlocks audio.
3. Paper lanterns float from the dock across the dark river.
4. Click on a drifting lantern to catch it; each caught lantern reveals a whispered prayer.
5. After all 10 lanterns are caught, the tide goes full-bright and the game ends.
6. Press "Release Again" to restart.
