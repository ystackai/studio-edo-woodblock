## What's Running

The 20-samurai Kawanakajima battlefield proof in a Three.js 3D browser game:

- **Scene:** Countryside battlefield with rolling terrain, distant hills, pine trees, field stones, war banners, and a central path
- **Actors:** 20 samurai (10 Takeda in red, 10 Uesugi in blue) loaded from Foundry GLB with pose variants, breathing/sway animations
- **Camera:** Orbit controls (drag + wheel zoom) + 6 presets (overview, red close, blue close, side profile, top formation, asset inspect)
- **Gameplay:** Charge (forward advance) / Reform (return to formation) with audio feedback
- **Lighting:** Cool near-white key, rim/kicker, ground fog, distant fog, PCFSoft shadows, ACES filmic tone mapping
- **Audio:** File-backed battlefield loop, charge cue, clash accent, formation step, UI confirm
