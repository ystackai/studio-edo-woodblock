# Preview

**Entry point:** `games/trial-p3-lantern-rain-a/index.html`

Open directly in a browser — fully self-contained, no server required.

## What to look for
1. Single paper lantern glowing in dusk rain
2. Film grain texture over everything
3. Touch/click to shield lantern — warm barrier appears
4. Hold to shield: flame grows steadier the longer you hold
5. Faint ring shows patience progress around lantern
6. Release and flame flickers again — but stays slightly steadier
