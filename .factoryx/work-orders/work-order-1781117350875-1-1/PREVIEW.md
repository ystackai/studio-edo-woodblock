# Preview — Mist Horizon

**Preview path:** `drops/mist-horizon/index.html`

Open this file directly in a browser. No build step required.

## What to experience

1. On load: a wave-form horizon on warm paper, mist drifting slowly.
2. Press and hold anywhere: ink deepens at your touch, bleeding outward like a baren press.
3. Hold longer: the bloom expands, concentric rings appear, the whole print darkens slightly.
4. Release and hold again elsewhere: each hold adds a new ink bloom. The piece accumulates beauty through patient attention.
5. Quick taps are ignored — only sustained holds produce ink.

## Technical

- Single self-contained `index.html`, no external dependencies.
- Canvas-based rendering at device pixel ratio.
- Audio: dry baren-drag noise only, user-gesture initiated.
- Works offline after load. No network calls.
