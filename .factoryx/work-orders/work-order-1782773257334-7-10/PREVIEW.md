# Preview — Edo Living Print

**Preview path:** `games/edo-living-print/`

**Self-contained single-file HTML game** — no external assets, no network dependencies.

## How to play

1. **Press and hold** anywhere on the canvas (mouse, touch, or spacebar/enter)
2. The living print **wakes** — the horizon rises, Fuji emerges, birds arrive
3. Keep holding to accumulate depth — new elements appear at each stage
4. **Release** and the print slowly fades back to paper
5. **Joy reveal:** at stage 4 (about 30s of pressing), a crane flies across the sky

## Five stages of the print

| Stage | Depth | What appears |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 0 | 0–0.18 | Paper grain + mist + hint text |
| 1 | 0.18–0.36 | Cloud wisps + Mount Fuji emerges |
| 2 | 0.36–0.54 | Boat with figure + cherry blossoms fall |
| 3 | 0.54–0.72 | Birds fly across the sky |
| 4 | 0.72–0.90 | Rain + a crane flies (joy reveal) |
| 5 | 0.90–1.0 | Full moon rises + everything at once |

The print **responds to where you press** — the horizon ripples at your touch point.
