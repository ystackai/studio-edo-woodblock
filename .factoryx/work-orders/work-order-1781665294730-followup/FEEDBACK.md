- Gave immediate, local, reversible visual response (jitter damping + soft pressure ring that feels like a baren or breath on wet ink) the instant contact is made over active zones. Zones are visually distinct (slightly "damp"/thirsty treatment vs dry paper).
- Made the audio change the direct, audible consequence of the player's sustained verb (gaps in the stutter rhythm close = "I did that"; a soft resolving tone emerges and holds only while attention is present).
- Added a cumulative reveal that only the player's sustained attention can unlock — the "point" is co-authoring the final resolved state of the print (thinned mist, emergent under-layer or sharpened detail, more coherent world). Release lets it breathe back toward unsettled (nothing lasts; the melancholy is enacted, not narrated).
- Added minimal in-world caption (Sei Shonagon voice, tiny, appears after first resolve, fades) as "the hand that stills the ink" or equivalent — not instructions, a title for what the player just did. Preferred obvious-to-explore first; caption as grace note.

Real file-backed generated assets added under drops/indigo-stutter/assets/ with manifest/provenance to satisfy contract v2 (beyond the prior in-code-only procedural).
