## v23 Intent

v23 is a realism-oriented iteration over v22. The generator changes were focused on reducing the toy/block read:

- Desaturated faction colors to reduce plastic primary-color intensity.
- Added garment-first under-kimono panels so the body reads less like stacked cubes.
- Added hakama cloth side panels and folds.
- Added armor lacing across the cuirass to create a more historical samurai surface rhythm.
- Reduced helmet bowl, brim, forehead exposure, and crest scale.
- Preserved v22's rectangular lamellae rather than reverting to the bead-like v21 armor.

## Verdict

This is useful evidence, but it is not a production-pass character set. The asset foundry path successfully generated paired `.glb`, `.blend`, still renders, turntable frames, and contact sheets with clean Blender logs. Visually, the proof remains stylized and toy-like, especially in head/helmet scale, limb construction, and simplified armor geometry. It should not be expanded to all twenty characters until the next proof improves the human silhouette and material realism enough to pass visual review.
