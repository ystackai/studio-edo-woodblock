# Preview: Quiet Opening — Lone Pine on Sea Cliff

**Path:** `games/trial-p2-quiet-opening-b/index.html`

**What it is:** A meditative, atmospheric art piece — no game, no tutorial, no prompts. Just a lone pine tree on a sea cliff shrouded in slowly drifting fog, rendered in a warm washi-paper ink wash style.

**Key behaviors:**
- Fog drifts slowly and organically across the scene
- Pine tree sways gently in the wind
- Sea waves ripple below
- Pointer/mouse movement subtly parts the fog, revealing more of the scene
- First screen is the finished piece — nothing to start, just observe

**Assets:** All procedural (canvas 2D). Zero external dependencies.
**Payload:** ~15KB single HTML file.
