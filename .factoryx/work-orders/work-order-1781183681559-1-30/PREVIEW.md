# Preview: lantern rain

**Preview path:** `games/trial-e1b-p3-lantern-rain-a/index.html`

## How to preview

Open the file directly in a browser:

```
file://<workspace>/games/trial-e1b-p3-lantern-rain-a/index.html
```

Or serve it locally:

```
python3 -m http.server 8080 -d .
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8080/games/trial-e1b-p3-lantern-rain-a/index.html`

## What to expect

- A single paper lantern hangs in the center of a dusk scene
- Rain falls diagonally across the screen
- The lantern has a warm, flickering glow with paper texture and grain
- **Tap/click and hold** anywhere on screen to shield the lantern from rain
- **Release** after a few seconds — the flame steadies and glows brighter as a reward for patience
- Release audio is user-initiated only (rain ambience starts on first tap)
- No score, no mechanics, no ending — just a quiet moment
