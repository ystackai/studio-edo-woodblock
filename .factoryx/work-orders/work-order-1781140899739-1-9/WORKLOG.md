## Design

A dusk scene: deep purple sky, distant silhouettes, faint moon. A paper lantern hangs center-frame with visible ribs, warm glowing flame, wet paper streaks, and water droplets running down. Rain falls continuously with wind angle. Hold near the lantern to create a shield dome that deflects rain. Over time, the flame steadies (less flicker, warmer glow) — patience is rewarded.

## Anchor Self-Review

- **Graphics: 4** — Detailed lantern with multiple glow layers, wet paper texture, rain ripples on wet ground, film grain, vignette. Screenshot-worthy.
