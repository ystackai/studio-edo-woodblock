# Preview: trial e1b/b — p1-living-print

## Live Preview
Open: `games/trial-e1b-p1-living-print-b/index.html`

## What you'll see
A warm paper-colored screen with a single deep-indigo wave undulating across the horizon. Mist drifts silently across the scene. The wave has a gentle rhythm — not too fast, not too slow.

## How to interact
- **Mouse:** Click and hold anywhere on the screen, then move your cursor. The wave deepens where you press.
- **Touch:** Press and hold anywhere, then drag.
- **Keyboard:** Hold Space bar — the press centers on screen.

The wave responds by darkening and deepening under your press, with ripple rings expanding outward. Release and the wave springs back. The mist also distorts where you press.

## Design intent
A living woodblock print. The piece exists already; you're just adding your mark to it, like a baren pressing into wet ink. No instructions needed — just press.
