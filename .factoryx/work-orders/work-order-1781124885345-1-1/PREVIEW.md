# Preview: Living Print (p1-living-print)

**Preview path:** `games/living-print/`

**What you see:** A single wave-form horizon rendered as an ink wash on warm paper grain. Mist drifts continuously across the surface. The piece opens as a complete, quiet statement — no loading, no instructions.

**What you do:** Press and hold anywhere (touch or mouse) or hold Space/Enter. The ink deepens slowly, like a baren press on a woodblock. There is slight resistance — the print does not respond instantly. The deeper you press, the more the horizon reveals itself. A ghost echo wave emerges at deep press. Release and the ink fades back.

**Audio:** A single, very low breathy tone on press. Rare mist-breath noise at deep hold. Near-silent otherwise.

**Size:** ~12 KB, no external dependencies.
