# Preview — Edo Moon Bridge Toy

## Playable Entry Point

`games/edo-moon-bridge-toy-canary-20260630t0454z/index.html`

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a browser
2. **Drag** on the screen to draw a brush-stroke path
3. The paper boat follows your path, carrying a lantern toward the moonlit bridge
4. **Deliver 3 lanterns** by guiding the boat to the bridge
5. Each delivery lights up a lantern on the bridge
6. After 3 deliveries: the moon rises, cherry blossoms fall — the bridge glows
