# Preview — Edo Moon Bridge

**Entrypoint:** `games/edo-moon-bridge-toy-canary-20260630t0454z/index.html`

**Preview URL:** Open the `index.html` directly in a browser or serve the `games/edo-moon-bridge-toy-canary-20260630t0454z/` directory.

**How to play:**
1. Open the page — a moonlit river scene appears with a small lantern boat
2. Drag your mouse/finger to draw a brush-stroke path toward the bridge (marked with 🌙)
3. The boat follows your path; if it reaches the bridge, a lantern is delivered
4. Deliver 3 lanterns to trigger the finale: the moon rises, cherry blossoms fall, and the bridge glows
5. Hit "Play again" to restart

**Design:** Edo woodblock style — paper grain overlay, ink-wash mountains, moonlit river with ripples, warm lantern glow, cherry blossom petals
