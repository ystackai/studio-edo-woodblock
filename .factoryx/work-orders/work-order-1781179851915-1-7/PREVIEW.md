# Preview — Koi Breath (trial e3/35b)

**Preview path:** `games/trial-e3-p4-koi-breath/index.html`

**How to play:**
1. Open the preview page in a browser
2. Press and hold (mouse click or touch) anywhere on the pond surface
3. Hold for at least 300ms to bloom ink — longer holds create larger, more complex ink patterns
4. Release to watch the ink bloom and fade
5. 7 koi swim around and will react to ink blooms

**Key interaction:** The dominant gesture is a patient press-and-hold. Quick taps do nothing — the piece rewards quiet persistence.
