# Preview — Rain on a Lantern

**Entrypoint:** `games/trial-p3-lantern-rain-a/index.html`

Open the HTML file in any modern browser. Fully self-contained, no server required.

**Scene:** A single paper lantern hanging beneath wooden eaves at dusk in the rain. Warm glow, wet paper, film grain.

**Interaction:** Touch or click to shelter the lantern with your hand. Hold to steady the flame.

**Audio:** "rain" button (bottom-right) toggles optional rain sound — user-initiated only.
