**Goal (verbatim from payload):**
Touch feels like pressing a baren or breathing on wet ink. Input response is physical and slightly resistant; frictionless swipe-through interaction kills the sense of craft. Frantic clicking is not rewarded; patience and repeated gentle engagement make the piece more beautiful.

## What This PR Contains

- **`games/ukiyo-e-printer/index.html`** — A complete, self-contained browser-based ukiyo-e printmaker experience:
  - Procedural washi paper texture background
  - Mt. Fuji silhouette with snow caps and drifting mist layers
  - Ink bloom system: clicking leaves organic, irregular-edge ink marks
  - Brushstroke drawing: drag to paint with soft ink bleed
  - **Baren press mechanic**: hold click to press ink into paper — at 60% depth a vermilion accent appears
  - **Saturation model**: rapid clicking degrades ink quality (frantic clicking is punished); patient, layered engagement produces richer prints
  - Three ink colors: sumi (ink), ai-zuri (indigo), beni (vermilion) — selected by position and saturation state
  - Ambient audio: procedural wind drone, brush-on-paper SFX, baren thud — all Web Audio API, starts only after user gesture
  - Finish button captures the canvas as PNG with a hand-seal (印) overlay, triggers download
  - Responsive canvas sizing, touch-friendly controls (≥44px targets)

## Preview

Open `games/ukiyo-e-printer/index.html` in a browser. Click/tap to leave ink blooms, drag to draw brushstrokes, hold to press the baren. Click the ♪ button for ambient audio. FINISH to download your print.

## Known Limitations

- Audio is procedural (Web Audio API oscillators and noise) — not generated by the Asset Foundry
- Single canvas scene — no layering of multiple prints
- No online gallery or sharing
